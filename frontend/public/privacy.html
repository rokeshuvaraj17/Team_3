Privacy Policy

MR_Rokesh Job Portal Assistant (Chrome Extension & Web App)

Last updated: August 20, 2026

MR_Rokesh Job Portal Assistant (“the Extension”, “we”, “our”) helps you save job postings and automatically fill job-application forms using information you have provided in your account. This policy explains what data the Extension handles and how it is used.

Information we handle

  • Account information you provide when you sign in (name, email) and your saved profile / resume details (such as phone, address, work authorization, skills, and resume text).
  • Job posting content from the page you choose to save (title, company, location, and description).
  • Form field labels on an application page, sent to our backend only to determine which of your saved values matches which field, so the form can be filled.

How we use the information

  • To fill job-application forms on your behalf when you explicitly trigger autofill.
  • To save jobs you choose to save and show them in your account.
  • To provide resume/cover-letter tailoring and job-tracking features you request.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not collect data from web pages unless you actively use a feature (save a job or run autofill).
  • We do not track your general browsing activity.

Data storage and sharing

Your account and profile data are stored on our backend database and are accessible only to your authenticated account. Application form labels sent for matching may be processed by an AI language-model provider solely to compute field matches; they are not used to identify you. We do not share your data with any other third parties except as required to operate these features or to comply with the law.

Data retention and deletion

Your profile and saved data are retained while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below, after which the data is removed from our systems.

Permissions

The Extension requests access to websites and the active tab only to read application-form fields and fill them, and to save job postings, at your request. These permissions are used strictly for the features described above.
