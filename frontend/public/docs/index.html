How-to Guides

How to generate a resume

/app/generate-resume
  1. Open Generate Resume from the sidebar.
  2. Pick a path: Job based (tailored to a specific job) or Resume Builder (LaTeX templates).
  3. For Job based: choose a job from your search or saved list; the app highlights relevant experience and skills.
  4. For Resume Builder: pick a template, then fill profile, education, work, skills, projects and awards across the 8 steps.
  5. Generate / export. Your resume is saved and appears in Documents.
  6. Open Documents to view or download it (PDF), or export as .tex / JSON from the builder.

You can

  • Create a job-tailored resume with AI
  • Build a resume from a LaTeX template step-by-step
  • Export as PDF, .tex, or JSON
  • Reuse profile data so fields are pre-filled

You cannot

  • Generate without connecting an AI provider key (API Connection) for AI flows
  • Use LaTeX templates other than Template 1 yet (others 'coming soon')
  • Edit a saved resume's raw content from the Documents page

How to generate a cover letter

/app/generate-cover-letter
  1. Open Generate Cover Letter from the sidebar.
  2. Step 1: pick a saved resume or paste resume text, then paste the Job description (required).
  3. Add Company name and Job title.
  4. Continue through the 4 steps and generate.
  5. The cover letter is saved and appears under Documents → Cover letters.

You can

  • Generate a cover letter from a resume + job description
  • Use resume text pulled from the extension, a saved resume, or pasted text
  • Save and download the result from Documents

You cannot

  • Generate without a job description (it is required)
  • Generate without an AI provider key connected
  • Send the cover letter to an employer from inside the app

How to autofill a job application

/app/autofill-form-fields
  1. Fill your details in Profile and in Autofill Form Fields (extra fields like EEO, salary).
  2. Install the Chrome extension from the Extension page and log in with the same account.
  3. Open a job application page on any site.
  4. Press Ctrl+Shift+F (Cmd+Shift+F on Mac) or use the extension to autofill.
  5. Optionally save the job to Job Tracker from the extension.

You can

  • Store every autofill value once and reuse across sites
  • Choose which fields autofill is allowed to fill/skip
  • Set voluntary EEO answers and salary expectations

You cannot

  • Autofill file uploads automatically on every site (resume upload is manual / drag-drop on some ATS)
  • Autofill without installing and logging into the extension

Getting Started

Sign In

/

Email + password sign-in to the portal. Remember-me and a link to sign up.

Step by step

  1. Go to the app URL. You'll land on the Sign In screen.
  2. Type the email address you registered with.
  3. Type your password. Use the eye icon to show/hide it if you want to check it.
  4. Optionally tick 'Remember me' to stay signed in on this device.
  5. Click 'Sign In'. On success you're taken straight to your Dashboard.
  6. New here? Click 'Sign up' at the bottom to create an account first.

Fields & controls

  • Email address — The email you registered with.
  • Password — Your account password; the eye icon toggles visibility.
  • Remember me — Keeps you signed in on this device.
  • Forgot password? — Link to recover access to your account.
  • Sign In button — Submits your credentials and opens the Dashboard.

You can

  • Sign in with email and password
  • Toggle password visibility and 'remember me'
  • Navigate to Sign up or Forgot password

You cannot

  • Sign in with wrong credentials (shows 'Invalid email or password')
  • Use Google sign-in (button present but not wired to a live OAuth flow)
  • Reach the dashboard without a valid account

Sign Up

/signup

Create a new account with name, email and password (min 8 characters).

Step by step

  1. From the Sign In screen, click 'Sign up'.
  2. Enter your full name (at least 2 characters).
  3. Enter a valid email address that isn't already registered.
  4. Choose a password with at least 8 characters.
  5. Submit to create your account.
  6. Continue into the short onboarding to add your profile basics (name, headline, location, phone).

Fields & controls

  • Name — Your full name (minimum 2 characters).
  • Email — A valid, unused email address.
  • Password — Minimum 8 characters.

You can

  • Register a new account (name, email, password)
  • Proceed into a guided onboarding for profile basics

You cannot

  • Register with a password under 8 characters
  • Reuse an email that already exists
  • Use an invalid email format

Job Search & Tracking

Dashboard (Job Search)

/app

Search jobs across multiple providers, filter and sort, view details, and save jobs.

Step by step

  1. Open the Dashboard (the home icon) from the sidebar.
  2. Choose a provider in 'Job source' (Arbeitnow, Adzuna, Jooble, Reed, or JSearch).
  3. Type a role or keyword in the search box (e.g. 'Frontend Developer').
  4. Optionally enter a city or location.
  5. Refine with the Country, Salary and Job type dropdowns, and set the sort order.
  6. Click 'Search'. Matching jobs appear under the 'All jobs' tab.
  7. Click any job to read the full details on the right.
  8. Save jobs you like; find them later under the 'Saved' tab.
  9. Use 'Clear filters' to reset everything and start over.

Fields & controls

  • Job source — Which job board the results come from. Different boards cover different regions/roles.
  • Keywords / role — Job title, role or keyword to match.
  • City or location — Where you want to work; leave blank for anywhere.
  • Country / Salary / Job type — Filters to narrow the results.
  • Relevance — Sort order for the results.
  • Search — Runs the query against the chosen provider.
  • All jobs / Saved tabs — Switch between search results and jobs you've saved.

You can

  • Search jobs by title/keyword and location
  • Switch job source provider (Arbeitnow, Adzuna, Jooble, Reed, JSearch)
  • Filter by country, salary and job type, and sort by relevance
  • Open a job to view details and switch between All jobs / Saved tabs
  • Save jobs for later

You cannot

  • See results before running a search (empty state until then)
  • Return results from a provider you haven't subscribed to (e.g. JSearch needs a RapidAPI subscription)
  • Submit an application without leaving to the employer's site

Profile & Account

Profile

/app/profile

Your details, profile photo and resume upload used across the app.

Step by step

  1. Open 'Profile' from the sidebar (or the account area).
  2. Under 'Basic info', set your Display name (required) — this is how you appear in the app.
  3. Add a Headline (e.g. 'Software Engineer') and a short Summary / bio.
  4. Note the Email is read-only here (it's your account login).
  5. In 'Location', pick your Country and optionally add City / Region and State.
  6. On the right, drag & drop or click to upload a Profile photo (PNG/JPEG/GIF/WebP).
  7. Also on the right, upload your Resume (PDF/DOC/DOCX) — it's used when you apply to jobs.
  8. Save your changes. These details pre-fill resumes, cover letters and autofill.

Fields & controls

  • Display name — Required. The name shown on your profile and documents.
  • Email — Read-only. Your account email / login.
  • Headline — A one-line professional title, e.g. 'Software Engineer'.
  • Summary — A short bio describing your experience and goals.
  • Country — Your country (used for job matching and forms).
  • City / Region — Optional city or region.
  • State — Optional state / province.
  • Photo upload — Profile picture. Allowed: PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP.
  • Resume upload — Your resume file. Allowed: PDF, DOC, DOCX. Used when applying.

You can

  • Edit display name, headline, summary and location
  • Upload a profile photo (PNG/JPEG/GIF/WebP)
  • Upload a resume (PDF/DOC/DOCX) used when applying

You cannot

  • Change the account email (read-only here)
  • Upload unsupported file types

Automation & Integrations

Browser Extension

/app/extension

Download and install the Chrome extension for autofill on job sites.

Step by step

  1. Open 'Extension' from the sidebar.
  2. Click 'Download extension' to get the packaged ZIP.
  3. Unzip the file on your computer.
  4. Open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions.
  5. Turn on 'Developer mode' (top-right toggle).
  6. Click 'Load unpacked' and select the unzipped 'dist' folder.
  7. The Team 3 icon appears in your toolbar. Log in with the same account.
  8. On any job page, press Ctrl+Shift+F (Cmd+Shift+F on Mac) to autofill.

Fields & controls

  • Download extension — Downloads the extension package (ZIP).
  • Install steps — Unzip → chrome://extensions → Developer mode → Load unpacked.
  • Autofill shortcut — Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+F fills forms on job pages once logged in.

You can

  • Download the packaged extension
  • Follow install steps (unzip → chrome://extensions → Developer mode → Load unpacked)
  • Use Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+F to autofill after logging in

You cannot

  • Install automatically (manual load-unpacked until it's on the Web Store)
  • Use it without logging in with the same account

API Connection

/app/api-connection

Connect AI provider API keys used for resume and cover-letter generation.

Step by step

  1. Open 'API Connection' from the sidebar.
  2. Choose your AI Provider (OpenAI, Gemini, etc.).
  3. Optionally add a Label (e.g. 'Work', 'Personal') to organize multiple keys.
  4. Paste your API key into the 'API key' field (the eye icon reveals it).
  5. Click 'Connect API key' to save it securely (only a masked version is shown afterward).
  6. Under 'Connected keys', set a default key used across the website and extension.

Fields & controls

  • Provider — The AI service the key belongs to (OpenAI, Gemini, etc.).
  • Label (optional) — A name to tell multiple keys apart.
  • API key — Your secret key; stored securely and shown masked after saving.
  • Connect API key — Saves the key to your account.
  • Connected keys — Your saved keys; choose which provider is the default.

You can

  • Add an API key per provider (OpenAI, Gemini, etc.) with an optional label
  • Set a default key used across the website and extension
  • Store multiple keys via labels; keys are stored masked

You cannot

  • See a full key again after saving (only a masked version is shown)
  • Share keys with other users
  • Generate AI content without at least one connected key

Autofill Form Fields

/app/autofill-form-fields

Every value autofill can use, pre-filled from Profile and fully editable.

Step by step

  1. Open 'Autofill Form Fields' from the sidebar.
  2. Review 'Personal & contact' — names, email, phone; many values come from your Profile.
  3. Fill 'Work authorization & EEO (voluntary)' only as much as you want (defaults are 'Prefer not to answer').
  4. Set your Salary expectations (min/max).
  5. Open 'Autofill settings' to choose which fields the extension is allowed to fill or should skip.
  6. These values are what the extension types into job application forms.

Fields & controls

  • Personal & contact — First/last name, preferred name, pronouns, email, phone (many from Profile).
  • Work authorization & EEO — Voluntary answers (sponsorship, veteran, disability, gender, race).
  • Salary — Your minimum and maximum salary expectations.
  • Autofill settings — Choose which fields autofill can fill or must skip.

You can

  • Edit personal & contact fields, work authorization and voluntary EEO answers
  • Set salary expectations
  • Choose which fields autofill is allowed to fill or skip

You cannot

  • Fill sensitive EEO fields unless you choose to (default 'Prefer not to answer')
  • Guarantee a match on every custom ATS field
